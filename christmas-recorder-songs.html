Which song should I start with?

Start with the Jingle Bells chorus: it uses just C5, D5, E5, F5 and G5. The other three settings reach the upper register and suit players who already know the basic scale.

For music class or home practice

These settings use Baroque soprano recorder fingerings. Check your instrument before starting: German and Baroque F fingerings differ. Learn the required notes first, play one phrase slowly, then join the phrases. Playback is a synthesized pitch guide, not a recorded recorder performance.
