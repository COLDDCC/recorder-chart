Common questions

Questions about the printable charts

Are the recorder fingering charts really free?

Yes. The charts and PDFs are free for teachers, parents and students — print them, photocopy them for a class, and hand them out. The only thing we ask is that you do not resell them.

Why is there a separate chart for each instrument?

Because the note names change. Soprano, tenor and great bass are C instruments (all holes covered gives C); alto, bass and sopranino are F instruments (the same fingering gives F). The finger patterns are shared within each family — only the names on the page differ.

How do I keep the chart to one page when printing?

Use the download button for the PDF version — it is already laid out as a single page. If you print from the browser, choose "print to one page" or scale 90% in the print dialog.
