Privacy policy

Last updated August 6, 2026. This policy is written to be short and honest: the recorder chart site collects essentially nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms other than email links, no comments, and no analytics scripts. We do not set tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share any data because there is no data to share.

How the site is hosted

This is a static site. The web host may keep routine server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and to keep the service running. We do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

Google Fonts

The pages load font files from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font, it connects to Google's servers, and Google may collect that request as described in Google's own privacy policy. You can prevent this by using a browser that blocks third-party requests.

Email contact

If you email us using the addresses on the chart pages, we keep your message only long enough to answer it. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we never publish your address.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top will move forward. If you have a question about it, email us at hello@recorder-chart.com.
