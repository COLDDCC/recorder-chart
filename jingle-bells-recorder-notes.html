Jingle Bells recorder notes

Complete chorus · Easy · C major · 4/4 · Baroque soprano recorder

Tap any note to hear it. Filled holes = covered; empty holes = open; half-filled thumb = pinch.

Read the notes: C5 is the lowest soprano note; C6 is one octave higher. Beat values use quarter notes: 0.5 = eighth note; 1.5 = dotted quarter; 2 = half note. Notes used: C5, D5, E5, F5, G5.

How to practise Jingle Bells

Practise the repeated E notes with a gentle “tu”. The C in the third phrase is low C5, below E5.

Start at 60 BPM. Listen to a phrase, stop playback and echo it on your recorder. The phrase breaks are practice chunks, not bar lines; follow the beat values for timing.
